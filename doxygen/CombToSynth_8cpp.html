Lower a signed division with a non-constant divisor to a restoring array divider operating on the magnitudes of the operands.

Returns the quotient and the remainder, rounded towards zero like comb.divs / comb.mods: the quotient is negative iff the signs of the operands differ, and the remainder takes the sign of the dividend.

◆ lowerUnsignedDivRem()

std::pair< Value, Value > lowerUnsignedDivRem ( OpBuilder & builder,
Location loc,
Value lhs,
Value rhs )
static

Lower an unsigned division with a non-constant divisor to a combinational restoring array divider.

Returns the quotient and the remainder.

The divider processes the dividend from MSB to LSB. In every step the partial remainder is shifted left by one, the next dividend bit is shifted in, and the divisor is trially subtracted. If the subtraction does not borrow, the quotient bit is one and the difference becomes the new partial remainder; otherwise the quotient bit is zero and the partial remainder is restored. The partial remainder is kept width + 1 bits wide so the borrow can be read off the MSB of the difference.

Division by zero yields an all-ones quotient and the dividend as remainder.
