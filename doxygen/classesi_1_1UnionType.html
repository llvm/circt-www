Detailed Description

Unions contain one active field without storing a tag.

All fields start at bit zero, and the width is the maximum field width. Serialization zero-pads the high bits of narrower fields, matching the hw.union bitcast layout. Nonzero hw.union member offsets are rejected during manifest generation.
