Parse the connection std::string and instantiate the accelerator.

Connection std::string format: <xclbin>[SEP<device_id>] wherein <device_id> is in BDF format (e.g. "0000:3b:00.1"). SEP is ':' on Unix and ';' on Windows to avoid conflicts with Windows drive letters (e.g. "C:\path\to.xclbin") and BDF colons.
